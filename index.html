About WayaTronic

Sri Lanka's Benchmark in Electronic Measurement

Founded by electronics and telecommunication engineers, WayaTronic was born to solve a critical challenge: providing Sri Lankan university labs, hardware startups, hobbyists, and industrial facilities with reliable, calibrated electronic test equipment at fair, direct-importer prices.

Every digital multimeter, oscilloscope, and laboratory DC supply in our catalog undergoes bench-verification before dispatch. Whether you are debugging high-frequency signals or building your first IoT robot, WayaTronic is your hardware ally.
